Set up CheaterFlag

Three quick steps. Green ticks mean you're done.

1. Sign in with Steam

A Steam window opens — sign in there. You type your password on Steam's own page; CheaterFlag never sees it.

2. Turn on the lobby scanner

CheaterFlag adds two settings to CS2 for you. Steam must be closed for a moment.

Do it myself instead

Copy this, click Open CS2 settings, and paste it into Launch Options:

-condebug -netconport 2121

3. Turn on after-game scanning (recommended)

Lets CheaterFlag find and scan every match you play, plus your recent older matches. Usually automatic after step 1.

Do it myself instead
  1. (Steam's official CS2 support page, in your browser — sign in if it asks)
  2. Under Access to Your Match History, click Create Authentication Code (skip if a code is already shown).
  3. Press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + C on that page.
  4. Come back and press

✅ All set!

CheaterFlag runs in the system tray and starts with Windows. Just play — you'll get a notification if something's wrong.

Current match

As soon as a match loads, CheaterFlag checks it against the shared blacklist. When Steam shows which game server you're on, blacklisted players in the same server are confirmed by SteamID — not just by name. Notifications wait until warmup or the menu, never mid-round. Every account is fully checked (bans + demo scan) right after the match.

Review queue

Strong-evidence flags from everyone's scans, newest match first. Watch the moments, then vote. A flag is decided early when 5+ members vote and 2/3 agree. Otherwise, on day 4 the majority of whatever votes it has decides — even one vote. No votes by day 5 = cleared (Valve deletes match demos after about 6 days, so decisions happen while everyone can still watch). Players already blacklisted are confirmed automatically, and decisions carry over when CheaterFlag updates its scanner. Members are scanned too — a confirmed member is suspended and blacklisted.

Shared blacklist

Members can vote to remove a wrong entry (needs 5+ votes with 2/3 agreeing, so one person can't undo it). Members can be blacklisted too (they're suspended when that happens). To report a player to Valve, report them once in CS2 from a match you played with them — CheaterFlag reminds you automatically.
